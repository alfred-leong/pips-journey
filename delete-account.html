Delete your account

How to delete your Pip's Journey account and everything saved with it.

What gets deleted

Your account (email and sign-in), the cloud backup of Pip's progress (step history, workouts, journey, postcards, outfits, medals and trip journal photos), your friend code and nickname, your friendships, cheers and shared flights. Copies in the database provider's routine backups expire within 30 days. Feedback you sent is kept but no longer linked to your account; ask if you'd like it deleted too.

Progress saved on your phone is not touched: clear it with Settings → Reset everything, or uninstall the app.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pip's Journey and sign in, if you aren't already.
  2. Go to the Me tab and tap the gear for Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then Delete my account twice to confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Send a request with the email address of your account. We'll email that address to confirm it's yours, then delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Or email journeywithpip@gmail.com from your account's address.
