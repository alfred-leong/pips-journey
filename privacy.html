Pip's Journey

Privacy Policy

The short version: your steps stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Pip's Journey is a walking app: a pixel-art godwit called Pip flies around the world on your steps. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores, where, and how to delete it. "We" means the developer of Pip's Journey. Questions: journeywithpip@gmail.com.

What the app reads

  • Steps and workouts. On Android, through Health Connect: your Steps and Exercise sessions (type, start and end time), including in the background so Pip keeps flying. On iPhone, through Apple Health: your Steps and Workouts. Your watch or fitness app (Samsung Health, Fitbit, Garmin Connect, Strava and others) reaches the app only that way. The app never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health in the released version.
  • Motion sensor, only in the web version, and only if you start step counting there.
  • Photos you pick for your trip journal. The app only sees the photo you choose.

The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID. Pip's position on the map is Pip's flight, worked out from your step count, not where you are.

What stays on your phone

Everything, by default. Your step history, workouts, Pip's journey, postcards, outfits, medals and trip journal are saved on your phone. You can use the app fully without an account.

If you create an account

An account is optional. It backs up your progress so it survives a new phone or a reinstall, and lets you add friends. With an account we store:

  • Your email address (and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the basic profile those services share: your email and an account ID). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • A backup of your progress: the same things the app saves on your phone, including daily step counts, workouts and your trip journal photos and captions.
  • For friends: a friend code, your nickname, and a summary your accepted friends can see: how Pip looks and where Pip is on the route, postcards and medals, and today's and this week's steps (you can turn steps off in Settings). Your trip journal is never shared with friends.

Feedback you send

If you use Send feedback, we store your message, the email you add (optional), your account ID if you're signed in, and basic app details (app version, Android, iPhone or web, screen size and the screen you were on) to help fix problems.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase hosts the accounts, backups, friends and feedback (our database provider).
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple".

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for advertising. There are no ads and no analytics trackers in the app.

Health data

Health data is used only to move Pip, show your steps and active minutes, and award in-app rewards. The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with third parties for those purposes, and leaves your phone only as part of your own account backup if you sign in.

You can turn off the app's access at any time in Health Connect's settings or in the Health app.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • On your phone: until you use Settings → Reset everything or uninstall the app.
  • In your account: until you delete the account. In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, backup, friend code, friendships and cheers straight away. Without the app, request deletion here.
  • Copies in the database provider's routine backups expire within 30 days.
  • Feedback messages are kept until they're dealt with, and deleted on request.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each signed-in person read and change only their own data, and their accepted friends' summaries.

Children

Pip's Journey is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app for anything significant.
