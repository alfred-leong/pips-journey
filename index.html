Pip's Journey

Every step flies Pip further.

Pip is a bar-tailed godwit: in real life, the bird that flies over 11,000 km from Alaska to New Zealand without stopping. In the app, Pip flies the world on your steps, collecting postcards from each place on the way.

How it works

  • Your steps come from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone, so any watch or fitness app works.
  • Pip flies a real route, landing at places along the way with a postcard for each.
  • Dress Pip up, earn medals, keep a trip journal, and fly alongside friends.
  • No ads. Your steps stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up.
